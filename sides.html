Baked Macaroni and Cheese

This is the mac and cheese that I make for family gatherings, hoildays, and anyday I need a warm comfort food after a long day. It's creamy, cheesy, and topped with cripsy goodness. Pairs well with a green veggie, and a lean protein. Prep time: 20 minutes | Cook time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 6 oz pasta, such as penne or elbow macaroni
  • 4 tbs butter
  • 3 tbs flour
  • 2 cups milk, the higher the fat, the richer
  • 4 oz Colby Jack Cheese
  • 4 oz Sharp Cheddar Cheese
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbs neutral oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Boil your pasta according to package instructions. I leave mine a little al dente so it doesn't get mushy while baking.
  2. Make a rue. In a sauce pot melt butter over medium/low heat. Add flour, stirring constantly to avoid clumps. cook for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Once smooth, season with salt, pepper, and garlic salt to taste. Slowly add milk a little at a time, stirring constantly and completely incorperating before adding more. Sauce should begin to thicken, continue to stir until desired consistency is reached. It should stick to the spoon but you shouldn't be able to dent the mixture or see the bottom of the pot.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in grated cheese until melted and smooth. Reserve 1 ounce of each cheese for topping.
  5. In a glass baking dish mix together noodles and cheese sauce. It should appear that there is too much sauce, but trust that there is no such thing.
  6. In a bowl, add panko breadcrumbs and your oil. Stir to hyrdrate panko so it will become crispy. Mix in remaining cheeses and season with salt and pepper. Top the pasta in the baking dish evenly with this mixture.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown and bubbly on top. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

Parmesan Roasted Brussels Sprouts

This simple recipe makes for a great green veggie option. Its salty and crispy but keeps some nutritional value. Prep time: 10 minutes | Cook time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Rinse Brussels sprouts and pat dry. Cut off the tops and halve each sprout.
  2. In a large bowl, toss the Brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder until evenly coated.
  3. Spread parmesan cheese across a nonstick pan.
  4. Spread the Brussels sprouts on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast for 25 minutes, or until crispy and golden brown.
  5. let cool for 5 minutes before serving. Enjoy!

Classic Mashed Potatoes

There is nothing revolutionary about these mashed potatoes. I have been making them since I was a teen and they pair well with any recipe that requires mashed potatoes, such as cottage pie. They also stand strong on their own. You can dress them up with different seasonings, sub the dairy for vegan options, or even lighter versions. This portion is for two people, so just double or triple for bigger parties.

Ingredients

  • 2 lrge or 3 medium Russet Poatatoes
  • 1/4 cup Milk
  • 1/4 cup butter or margarine
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Optional Garlic Powder

Instructions

  1. Wash your potatoes and remove peel if desired. Peeled means smoother and creamier mashed potatoes.
  2. Dice the potatoes into evenly sized pieces and add them to a pot of salty water. Bring to a boil and cook until tender. 10-15 mins. Once it's easy to break potatoes apart with a fork, drain potatoes and return to the pot.
  3. Mash potatoes until no lumps are left. You can use a classic potato masher, but I prefer a hand mixer to save time and mix everything smoothly.
  4. Add milk first and fully incorporate. You can adjust the thickness of your potatoes by adding more or less milk to meet your preference.
  5. Now add butter and mix until melted and smooth. Salt and pepper to taste. You shouldn't have to add much salt if your boiling water was salty enough, so definitely taste at this step. Add optional additional seasonings

Butter Steeped Carrots

These carrots are a simple and delicious. A great way to add a veggie to your meal that even the veggie hater will enjoy. I often have this as a lunch but it pairs well with any protien. Prep time: 1 minute | Cook time: 15-20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pound of baby carrots
  • 2 tbs butter
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Garlic powder or Kinder Buttery Garlic Seasoning

Instructions

  1. In a nonstick sautee pan add carrots and butter. Season liberally. Sometimes I add a splash of water to help steam the carrots quicker.
  2. Cover and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent burning. Some browning is great.
  3. Once fork tender, remove from heat and serve.

Garlic Green Beans

These are my take on the delicious green beans fron Din Tai Fung. They are quick, delicious, and pair well with anything.

Ingredients
  • Bag of green beans
  • Avocado Oil
  • 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and Pepper

Instructions

  1. Rinse and trim the green beans if necessary.
  2. in a large skillet on medium heat, add avocado oil and minced garlic. Cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add green beans to the skillet and toss to coat in the garlic and oil. Add Salt and pepper. You can add a splash of water to steam. Cover and cook for 5-7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the green beans are tender but still crisp. Be mindful of burning the garlic and lower the heat if necessary.
  4. Serve with your protein or main of choice.
