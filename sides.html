Baked Macaroni and Cheese

This is the mac and cheese that I make for family gatherings, hoildays, and anyday I need a warm comfort food after a long day. It's creamy, cheesy, and topped with cripsy goodness. Pairs well with a green veggie, and a lean protein. Prep time: 20 minutes | Cook time: 25 minutes

  • 6 oz pasta, such as penne or elbow macaroni
  • 4 tbs butter
  • 3 tbs flour
  • 2 cups milk, the higher the fat, the richer
  • 4 oz Colby Jack Cheese
  • 4 oz Sharp Cheddar Cheese
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbs neutral oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder
  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Boil your pasta according to package instructions. I leave mine a little al dente so it doesn't get mushy while baking.
  2. Make a rue. In a sauce pot melt butter over medium/low heat. Add flour, stirring constantly to avoid clumps. cook for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Once smooth, season with salt, pepper, and garlic salt to taste. Slowly add milk a little at a time, stirring constantly and completely incorperating before adding more. Sauce should begin to thicken, continue to stir until desired consistency is reached. It should stick to the spoon but you shouldn't be able to dent the mixture or see the bottom of the pot.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in grated cheese until melted and smooth. Reserve 1 ounce of each cheese for topping.
  5. In a glass baking dish mix together noodles and cheese sauce. It should appear that there is too much sauce, but trust that there is no such thing.
  6. In a bowl, add panko breadcrumbs and your oil. Stir to hyrdrate panko so it will become crispy. Mix in remaining cheeses and season with salt and pepper. Top the pasta in the baking dish evenly with this mixture.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown and bubbly on top. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.

Parmesan Roasted Brussels Sprouts

This simple recipe makes for a great green veggie option. Its salty and crispy but keeps some nutritional value. Prep time: 10 minutes | Cook time: 25 minutes

  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 2 tbs olive oil
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder
  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Rinse Brussels sprouts and pat dry. Cut off the tops and halve each sprout.
  2. In a large bowl, toss the Brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder until evenly coated.
  3. Spread parmesan cheese across a nonstick pan.
  4. Spread the Brussels sprouts on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast for 25 minutes, or until crispy and golden brown.
  5. let cool for 5 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
